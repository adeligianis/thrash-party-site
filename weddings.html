How the Night Runs

    Entrances & first dance

    The wedding party comes in and you take the floor for your first dance.

    Dinner & toasts

    Set one

    1hr+ of crowd-tested songs. The dance floor opens here.

  5. 9:00 pm

    Set two

    Another 1hr+, ending with the singalongs. Last dance, then "Sweet Caroline" if you'll let us.

FAQ

Questions couples ask

How long do you play?+–

Your call. We typically do two sets of 1hr+ each, with playlists covering the breaks.

Can we pick the songs?+–

Of course! Pick from the full list, mark anything you don't want played, and request your first dance and parent dance songs.

Will you learn a song for us?+–

We'll be happy to learn new songs for you! Ask us about your first dance song. For songs that don't suit a live band, we'll add them to the break playlist instead.

Do you take requests from guests?+–

Your call. We can take requests on the night, or send them to you first.

Do you bring sound and lights?+–

Yes. Our own PA, stage lights and uplighting, run by our own engineer.

What will the band wear?+–

Our standard wedding attire is all-black business attire but we will coordinate with you to match your vibe.

Do you only play weddings?+–

No. We play birthdays, anniversaries, backyard parties, fundraisers and corporate events across New England.

Can we see you live first?+–

Yes! We play bars around Boston, Providence and New Hampshire most months. Check upcoming gigs and come say hi.

Do you travel?+–

We're based near Boston and play all of New England.
